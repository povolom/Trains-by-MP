Your GO train trips, straight into your calendar.

Pick the trains you take and get calendar entries for them, so your commute sits next to your classes.

Schedule data will come from GO Transit's free open data, used under Metrolinx's terms.

How I'm building it

  1. Write a one-page brief: who it is for, the problem, how I will measure success and what is out of scope.
  2. Survey TMU commuters about what actually slows them down.
  3. Build the smallest version that helps: pick a trip, get a calendar entry.
